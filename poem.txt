The Silent Watcher of the Night

Upon the hill where shadows creep,
And ancient oaks in silence sleep,
The moon ascends her silver throne,
To claim the kingdom as her own.
The stars ignite their distant fires,
To warm the world of sleeping desires,
And cast a glow on fields of grain,
That whisper soft against the rain.

The river winds through valleys deep,
Where secrets of the past must keep
Their stories written in the stone,
And carved by time, and carved by bone.
The water rushes, cold and clear,
To find the ocean's distant sphere,
Where waves collide with sandy shores,
And crash against the rocky floors.

Oh, time, you thief of youth and cheer,
You steal the moment, make it dear,
Then vanish like a summer breeze,
Leaving only the passing trees.
But in the heart, the memory stays,
Of golden days and misty days,
Of laughter loud and weeping low,
Of all the things we used to know.

The seasons turn their endless wheel,
From winter's frost to summer's zeal,
From autumn's gold to spring's green birth,
A cycle of the earth and earth.
The flowers bloom, the leaves fall down,
The sun and moon go round and round,
And we, mere mortals, stand and gaze,
At mysteries that time amazes.

Yet in the night, when all is still,
And stars paint patterns on the hill,
I feel a peace, a quiet grace,
That fills my soul with sweet embrace.
For in the dark, the light is found,
In silence, peace is to be found,
And in the heart, a fire burns,
That every hope and dream returns.

So let the world spin on its way,
Let night give place to golden day,
For every end is but a start,
A brand new beat within the heart.
And though the years may slip away,
Like sand that falls through broken clay,
The spirit soars, the mind takes flight,
Into the vast and endless night.
